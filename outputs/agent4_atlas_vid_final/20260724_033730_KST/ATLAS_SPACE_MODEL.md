# Atlas Space Model

## S1 Editorial Time Space

Evidence Line and Story chapters preserve request → question → answer → result → verification order. Atlas does not replace that rhythm.

## S2 Topic Space

The canonical Story and Explorer node positions are approved UMAP 2D display coordinates. The warning is mandatory: “이 지도는 의미적 위치를 2차원으로 투영한 표시용 공간입니다. 2차원 거리 자체를 실제 유사도 점수로 해석하지 마십시오.”

## S3 Behavior Space

Behavior is encoded only through family shape and A-code inner marks, supported by text labels. A1–A4 are information/non-direct, A5–A6 are deferral/procedural, A7–A8 are action/evidence. The complete A1–A8 set is not described as avoidance.

## S4 Projection Method Space

PCA, UMAP, optional 3D, and Tensor are opt-in methodological views. Their coordinates are not absolute cross-method coordinates. PCA axes may be PC1/PC2/PC3 with explained variance; UMAP dimensions are not assigned semantic names.
